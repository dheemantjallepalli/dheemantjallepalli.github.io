03 — About Me & Contact

I decode movement from muscle.

I'm a Research Fellow at the Harvard Bionics Lab, working where neural engineering, wearable biosensing and human–machine interfaces meet. I decode neuromotor intent from surface EMG to drive neuroprosthetics, exoskeletons and interactive rehabilitation, with the goal of restoring motor function after neurological injury.

I'm also interested in closed-loop neurorehabilitation, pairing biosignal decoding with neuromodulation and spinal cord stimulation to promote recovery. Away from the bench: football (I captained my school team), middle-distance running, and too many photos of light installations.
